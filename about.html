About Us

The Story Behind
Vidhyasthali Library

Vidhyasthali Library was established in Barmer with a single, clear mission: to provide every student and competitive exam aspirant with a permanent, world-class study sanctuary right here in Laxmi Nagar.

Located in our officially owned premises near Sankalp Classes, we purpose-built this library from the ground up for serious self-study. We equipped the study hall with 32+ private numbered desk cubicles, high-grade air conditioning, high-speed fiber WiFi, purified RO drinking water, continuous power backup, and round-the-clock CCTV security.

This is a place where students preparing for REET, RAS, RPSC, SSC, Banking, Defense, NEET, JEE, and Board exams can leave all distractions behind. Every morning from 6:00 AM until 11:00 PM at night, our hall remains in disciplined, pin-drop silence — giving every learner the comfort, focus, and dignity they deserve.

Alongside our physical reading desks, we created our free digital library collection. It offers classic Hindi and English literature for anyone who wants to enrich their mind, whether studying at their reserved desk or reading at home.

Vidhyasthali is an investment in Barmer's future. One desk, one book, and one step towards success at a time.
